Mesa lets people play physical Magic: The Gathering with friends who are somewhere else. A phone camera over the table reads the real cards as they are played and mirrors them onto a shared digital table, so everyone sees the same board. Each player's hand and library stay private. Players can also use the same shared table without a camera, placing cards by hand. Mesa is a web app that runs in the browser on phone and desktop. Players sign in, add their decks, and start or join a game with a code or a link. It is currently in private beta.
