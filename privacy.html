5. How long we keep it

Your account, content and usage events are kept for as long as you have an account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and content, your reports and pictures, and ask Mixpanel to delete your events. The count of AI questions is kept without any link to you. Images sent for AI help are not stored by us unless they are part of a report or a correction, as in section 2.

Problem reports, error reports and pictures of cards you correct are deleted after 90 days, unless we have used them to fix a problem or to improve card recognition: as a test case that checks a problem stays fixed, or to train a model. Those are kept until you delete them or your account.

Pictures already used to train a model can't be taken out of that model. When you delete them, they are deleted from Mesa, and no new model is trained on them.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, or to move it to another service. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]; we answer within one month.

You can also do two things yourself, in Settings on Home. Delete my reports and pictures deletes every report and picture you have sent. Turning off Share pictures of cards I correct stops new pictures from being kept from that moment; it does not delete the ones already kept.

We may write to you about a report you sent.

7. Objecting to usage events and error reports

You can object to us recording usage events and error reports at any time, without giving a reason. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we stop sending events and error reports for your account and delete the ones we have.

8. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

9. Age
